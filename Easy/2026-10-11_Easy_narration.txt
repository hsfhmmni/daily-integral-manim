Easy Integral - Sunday 11 October 2026

1. How big is the green area from x equals 1 to x equals 2?
2. Thin rectangles estimate the area; watch the gap shrink.
3. Factor the bottom, and the logarithm on top splits too.
4. log (x(x plus 1)) becomes log x plus log (x plus 1).
5. Group the log (x plus 1) terms into (x plus 1)log (x plus 1).
6. Split the fraction: each term cancels one factor of the bottom.
7. The Product Rule
8. (f g)' equals f' g plus f g'
9. Here f equals log x and g equals log (x plus 1).
10. The Chain Rule
11. d over dxf (g(x)) equals f' (g(x)) g'(x)
12. g' equals 1 over x plus 1 times 1 gives the term log x over x plus 1.
13. That is the product rule for log x times log (x plus 1).
14. Integrating a derivative undoes it.
15. At x equals 2: log 2 times log 3. At x equals 1: log 1 times log 2 equals 0.
16. More, thinner rectangles close in on log 2 times log 3 about 0.7615.
17. Takeaway: when terms pair up as f'g plus fg', integrate to fg.
