4. Its derivative is already in the integral, so substitute.

8. In u: the curve is cos inverse u, and the area is unchanged.
9. Use the identity cos inverse u equals pi over 2 minus sine inverse u.
10. The constant pi over 2 over a width of 2 gives pi over 2 times 2.
11. sine inverse u is odd: its two pieces are mirror images.
12. Spun about the origin, they cancel: signed area 0.
13. So the inverse sine part contributes nothing.
14. More, thinner rectangles close in on pi.
15. Takeaway: when a function's derivative sits beside it, substitute.
