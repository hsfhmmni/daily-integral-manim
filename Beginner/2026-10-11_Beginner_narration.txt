4. Linearity of the Integral
5. (a f plus b g) dx equals a\! f dx plus b\! g dx
6. f, g are functions; a, b are constants.
7. A sum, or a constant factor.

12. f (g(x)) g'(x) dx equals f(u) du, u equals g(x)
13. u equals g(x) is the inside; du equals g'(x) dx.
14. An inside function appears with its derivative.

17. The Power Rule
18. x to the n dx equals x to the n plus 1n plus 1 plus C, n minus 1
19. n is fixed; C is any constant.
20. A power of the variable, like t cubed.
21. Differentiating x to the n plus 1n plus 1 gives back x to the n.
22. With n equals 6: u to the 6 du equals u to the 77; put back u equals x plus 1.
23. Raise each power by one, then divide by 7.
24. The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
25. _a to the b f(x) dx equals F(b) minus F(a), F' equals f
26. F is any antiderivative of f.
27. A definite integral, once an antiderivative is known.
28. Area grows at the rate of the curve's height.
29. At x equals 1: 2 to the 77 minus 1 over 7. At x equals 0: 1 over 7 minus 0.
30. Upper limit minus lower limit.
31. 2 to the 7 equals 128, and the two 1 over 7 terms combine.
32. 128 minus 2 over 7 equals 126 over 7 equals 18.
33. More, thinner rectangles close in on 18.
34. Takeaway: a shifted power like (x plus 1) to the 6 integrates like x to the 6.
